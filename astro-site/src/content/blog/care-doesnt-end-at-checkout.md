The visit may be over, but the patient's care journey continues.

Healthcare is a journey, not just a visit.

A patient's experience with a clinic does not begin and end inside the consultation room. From the first appointment to patient records, consultation, prescriptions, investigations, billing, and follow-ups, every step contributes to the overall care journey.

When these steps are disconnected, healthcare teams may spend unnecessary time managing information, tracking follow-ups, and coordinating what happens next.

OneView connects the journey.

## Treatment Plans Keep Care Moving Forward

After meeting with a doctor, a patient may need to follow a treatment plan, take prescribed medications, complete investigations, review reports, or return for another consultation.

OneView helps keep these next steps connected to the patient record.

## Follow-ups Are Part of the Journey

Follow-up appointments can be an important part of monitoring progress, reviewing results, updating treatment plans, or continuing a patient's care.

OneView helps clinics organize upcoming follow-ups and connect them with the patient's previous visits and treatment history.

## Reminders Make the Next Step Clear

OneView helps healthcare teams organize reminders, including:

- Appointment reminders
- Medication reminders
- Investigation reminders
- Report review reminders
- Follow-up reminders

## From Consultation to Continued Care

**Consultation → Treatment Plan → Prescription → Investigation → Results → Follow-up → Continued Care**

Each step builds on the previous one.

## Better Continuity of Care

Good healthcare is not only about what happens during a consultation. It is also about what happens afterward.

OneView helps keep this information connected, giving healthcare teams a more organized way to manage ongoing patient care.

## One Platform. Complete Patient Care. Better Health.

OneView connects appointments, patient records, consultations, prescriptions, investigations, billing, and follow-ups into one platform.

Because every visit is part of something bigger.

The consultation may end. The care continues.
